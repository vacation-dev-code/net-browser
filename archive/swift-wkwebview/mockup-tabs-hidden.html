The quiet browser: less chrome, more page

Normal browsing — tabs hidden, top bar barely there.

This is the default state. No tab strip, no address bar, no toolbar. Just a 28-point glass bar at the top with back, forward, and the three-dot menu, and the full width of the window for the page itself.

Hover the right edge and the vertical tabs slide out. Move away and they disappear again — the page never reflows.

That is the whole interface. Everything else is one menu or one keyboard shortcut away.
